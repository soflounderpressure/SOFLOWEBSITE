Why we soft wash a pool cage

Most pool homes in Miami-Dade and Broward have a screen enclosure, also called a pool cage or lanai screen. Humidity, afternoon rain, shade from nearby trees and pollen all collect on the mesh, so many enclosures turn green or dark long before the rest of the house looks dirty.

Many cleaners advise against high pressure on screens. It can tear the mesh and pop the splines, the rubber cord that holds the screen in its frame. A soft wash uses low pressure and a cleaning solution that does the work instead, then a careful rinse.

The same goes for the aluminum frame. We clean the frame and the screen in the same pass, so the whole cage looks even from the pool deck and from the street.

What gets cleaned

Screen panels. Algae, mildew, pollen film and the gray dust that clogs the mesh and dulls the view.

Aluminum frames and rails. Chalky oxidation, dirt streaks and the green growth that sits in the corners.

Lanai and pool deck. Not included in the screen price, but easy to add in the same visit. See pool deck cleaning.

How often

Once or twice a year is a common schedule for a pool cage in South Florida. Enclosures under trees or in heavy shade tend to need it more often, and screens that stay dry and sunny can go longer.

Screen Enclosure Cleaning - FAQ

How much does screen enclosure cleaning cost in Miami?

It starts at $139 for a small enclosure. Larger or taller cages and heavier buildup cost more, and prices are set by area, so Broward runs a little higher than Miami-Dade. The instant quote shows your exact number, and the flat price is confirmed before we schedule.

Can you pressure wash a pool screen enclosure?

Many cleaners advise against it. High pressure can tear the mesh and pop the splines out of the frame, and torn screens mean a rescreen. We soft wash instead, with low pressure and a solution that lifts the growth without forcing water through the mesh.

How often should a pool cage be cleaned?

Once or twice a year is common in South Florida. Shaded enclosures, or ones close to trees and landscaping, often need it more often. If the screens look gray, green or spotted from the pool deck, it is time.
