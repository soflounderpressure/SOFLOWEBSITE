What We Clean

Sidewalks and entryways - the highest-impact surface, and the first thing every customer walks across.

Storefront glass frames, mullions and doors - the frames and thresholds, where grime actually accumulates.

Awning frames and undersides - fabric awnings are soft washed only.

Exterior walls and columns - soft washed if stucco or painted.

Dumpster pads and service areas - hot water and degreaser; the most-neglected area on most commercial properties and frequently a code issue.

Drive-throughs and loading zones.

Grease and Gum Need Hot Water

This is the practical difference between residential and commercial work.

Sidewalks outside food service accumulate grease, which cold water simply moves around. Hot water emulsifies it so it actually lifts. Chewing gum is the same story - cold pressure spreads it into a grey smear, hot water and the right tip removes it cleanly.

If you've had a sidewalk cleaned before and the dark patches came straight back, it was almost certainly cleaned cold.

Who This Is For

Retail storefronts and strip plazas, restaurants and cafés with sidewalk seating, offices and medical suites, salons and gyms, and property managers handling multiple units. For multi-unit properties a quarterly rotation generally costs less per visit and keeps the whole plaza consistent, which matters when one dirty unit drags the look of the row down.

HOA, Plaza and Municipal Requirements

Many Miami-Dade and Broward plazas require tenants to maintain their own frontage, and some municipalities have sidewalk cleanliness ordinances around food service. If you've received a notice, send it to us - it usually specifies exactly what has to be addressed and by when, and we'll work to that date.

Commercial Storefront Cleaning - FAQ

Do I have to close my business while you clean?

No. Commercial work is scheduled early morning before opening or after close. Entryways stay passable, hoses are routed clear of doors, and we are finished and gone before your first customer arrives.

How much does commercial pressure washing cost in Miami?

Commercial work is custom quoted rather than list-priced, because square footage, surface type, grease and gum load, and access vary enormously. Recurring contracts cost significantly less per visit than one-off cleans. Call (305) 894-6336 or send details through the quote form for a flat number.

Can you remove gum and grease from a sidewalk?

Yes, with hot water. Cold pressure spreads gum into a grey smear and simply moves grease around, which is why previously cleaned sidewalks often look dirty again within days. Hot water emulsifies grease so it lifts properly and removes gum cleanly.

How often should a commercial storefront be cleaned?

Quarterly suits most retail and office frontage. Food service and high-footfall locations generally need monthly or bi-monthly attention on the sidewalk, because grease and gum accumulate continuously rather than seasonally.

Do you work with property managers on multiple units?

Yes. For strip plazas and multi-unit properties a quarterly rotation across all units costs less per visit and keeps the whole row consistent, which matters because a single neglected frontage drags down the appearance of the entire plaza.
