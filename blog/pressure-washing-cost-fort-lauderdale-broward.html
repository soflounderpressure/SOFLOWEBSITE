What Broward jobs usually involve

Most Broward homes we hear about have the same short list of surfaces: a concrete or paver driveway, a pool deck, a screened enclosure, painted stucco walls, and a roof. In the humid wet season the north and shaded sides of a home often go green first, and many Broward neighborhoods are in HOA communities that check roofs and driveways. If you have a letter in hand, read what Florida HOAs can require before you schedule.

What changes a quote

Size. A medium driveway, patio, house or roof is priced at 1.5 times the small price, a large one at 2.1 times, and an extra-large one at 2.8 times. Walkways and fences scale up more slowly.

Buildup. Moderate buildup adds 25 percent and heavy buildup adds 55 percent. Heavy oil or rust stains add $50 on a first clean.

Access. Two-story homes cost 30 percent more for the house wash and 15 percent more for the roof. Tight side yards and landscaping crowding the walls add time too.

Tile roof. Tile roofs cost 30 percent more than the shingle price. See the roof soft washing cost guide for examples.

Bundling. Extra surfaces in the same visit are priced at 60 percent, with the most expensive surface at full price. As an example from our quote engine, a small driveway plus a small house soft wash in zone C comes to about $385 instead of $438. That is an example, not a quote.

Common questions

Why does Fort Lauderdale cost more than Hialeah?
Our rates are set by area. Fort Lauderdale is in our premium-area rate, 15 percent over the standard Miami rate that Hialeah gets. The instant quote shows the exact rate for your address.

Is the price the same for every home in a city?
No. The table shows starting prices for a small surface with light buildup. A bigger driveway, heavier growth, a two-story house, or a tile roof raises the number, and we confirm a flat price before we schedule.

Do you serve Broward cities that are not listed here?
We serve Miami-Dade and Broward. If your city is not in the table, the instant quote prices by ZIP code, so enter your address or ZIP to see your rate.
