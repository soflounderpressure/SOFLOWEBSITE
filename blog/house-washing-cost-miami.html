Pricing guide

How Much to Pressure Wash a House Exterior in Miami

A full house exterior soft wash at SoFlo starts at $279. Prices are set by area, and the instant quote shows yours. What takes it up from there is size, height, and how much growth is on the walls. Here is the honest breakdown.

Your house should be soft washed, not blasted

This is the single most important thing to understand before you compare quotes. Stucco is porous by design, and paint sits on top of it. High pressure forces water into the wall and can blow out sections of finish or strip paint outright. The correct method for walls is soft washing: low pressure and a cleaning solution that kills the algae and mildew, then rinses away.

So if one quote is cheap because they are running a pressure washer across your stucco, that is not a deal. That is damage you pay for twice.

What moves the number

Size and stories. A single-story ranch is quicker and safer to reach than a two-story with tall gable walls. Height adds equipment and time.

Growth. North-facing and shaded walls hold moisture and green up first. A house that has not been washed in two years takes more product than one on a maintenance cycle.

What is attached. Screen enclosures, soffits, and heavy landscaping against the walls all add to the scope.

What a fair quote includes

A real house-wash quote should say what surfaces are covered and what method each gets. Walls and soffits soft washed, driveway and walkway pressure washed, screen enclosure if you have one. See how we handle it on the house soft washing page, or get a quote in a few taps for a flat number.

Common Questions

Is the whole house pressure washed?
No, and this matters. Stucco, paint, and siding get soft washed at low pressure. Only hard surfaces like the driveway and walkway get true pressure washing. A company that pressure washes your stucco is damaging it.

What does a house wash include?
A proper house wash covers the walls, soffits, fascia, and usually the entryway. Many Miami homes bundle the driveway and screen enclosure in the same visit, which is cheaper than booking them separately.

Will it get rid of the green and black stains on my walls?
Yes. Those stains are algae and mildew, and soft washing kills them at the root rather than just rinsing the surface. That is why the walls stay clean far longer than a quick pressure rinse.
