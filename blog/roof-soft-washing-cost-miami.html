Roof Soft Washing Cost in Miami: Tile vs Shingle

Updated November 2026

Roof soft washing at SoFlo UnderPressure starts at $349. That is a starting price for a small roof at our standard Miami rate, not a typical price for every roof. Tile roofs cost 30 percent more, and larger, steeper or dirtier roofs cost more again. A real roof needs a look or a few photos before we can give you a flat quote.

What moves the roof price

Roof size and pitch. In our quote engine a medium roof is priced at 1.5 times the small price, a large roof at 2.1 times, and an extra-large roof at 2.8 times. A steeper slope adds time and difficulty. A low, walkable roof is faster than a steep barrel-tile roof that has to be treated from a ladder and the ground.

Tile versus shingle. Barrel tile is common in Miami and it is fragile. It cannot be walked casually, so the work is slower and more careful. Shingle is more forgiving but needs the same gentle chemistry so the granules stay put. That care is why tile adds 30 percent.

How far gone it is. Moderate buildup adds 25 percent and heavy buildup adds 55 percent in our quote engine. Light streaking rinses faster than a roof that has gone solid black and green.

Stories. A two-story home adds 15 percent to the roof price.

Why a real roof needs a look

A starting price cannot see your pitch, how many roof sections you have, how much of the tile is lifted or cracked, or how heavy the growth is. Send a few clear photos through the quote page and we confirm a flat price before we schedule. If the photos do not show enough, we will say so instead of guessing.

Why the cheapest quote deserves a question

A very low roof quote in Miami is often someone planning to pressure wash it. That is the fast, cheap way, and it can strip shingle granules, crack or shift tile, and force water under the tile. The Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association's own guidance is low-pressure cleaning only.

So the real comparison is not $349 versus a cheaper number. It is a low-pressure soft wash versus a pressure wash that can cost you a roof section later. Ask any roof quote one question: what PSI are you running up there? If it is anything near driveway pressure, keep looking.

Common questions

Why is roof cleaning more than driveway cleaning?
A roof is slower, higher-risk work. It is done from a ladder or the ground with long-reach equipment, uses a cleaning solution rather than just water, and the crew is working around tile that cracks if you walk it wrong. The care is the cost.

Should I just pressure wash the roof to save money?
No. High pressure can strip granules off shingles, crack or displace tile, and push water under it. Many roof cleaners and roofers recommend low-pressure soft washing instead. If your roof is under a manufacturer warranty, check its terms before anyone cleans it.

How long does a clean roof stay clean?
It depends on shade, nearby trees, and how much rain the roof sees, so we cannot promise a number of years. Some roofing guides suggest cleaning a tile roof every 2 to 3 years. A recurring plan keeps regrowth from building up.
