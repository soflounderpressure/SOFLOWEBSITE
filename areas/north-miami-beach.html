Why North Miami Beach Needs It

Two Climates
In One City

North Miami Beach is really two maintenance environments. Inland, the mid-century housing stock off Biscayne and through the residential grid deals with standard South Florida algae on concrete and stucco. Out toward Eastern Shores and the canal-front streets, salt air and constant humidity off the water accelerate everything - those properties generally need attention twice as often as their neighbors a mile inland.

South Florida Climate

Miami-Dade and Broward average over 60 inches of rain per year. Humidity and warmth keep algae, mold, and mildew growing on exterior surfaces year-round.

Mixed Housing Stock

A lot of original mid-century construction here, with older painted surfaces and terrazzo entries that do not take pressure well. Soft washing is the default on anything painted or original.

Surface Types

Concrete drives and walkways, flat tile and shingle roofs, painted block and stucco, seawalls and dock areas on the canal streets, and screened patios. Waterfront properties carry salt film on top of ordinary algae.

Pressure washing in North Miami Beach: what's different here

North Miami Beach is mostly inland, but part of it reaches the water. Eastern Shores, on the Intracoastal side, has canal-front streets where salt and humidity come off the water all year. Neighborhoods like Highland Village sit well to the west, with smaller homes, and they deal with ordinary algae rather than salt.

That split matters for how often a property needs attention. A seawall and a dock get green quickly. A driveway three streets inland is on a slower clock, though not a slow one.

How much does pressure washing cost in North Miami Beach?

Pressure washing in North Miami Beach starts at $99 for a driveway, $79 for a walkway, $129 for a patio or pool deck and $99 for a fence. A house soft wash is $249 and a roof soft wash is $299. Waterfront homes usually cost more because they are larger and carry salt film on top of algae. Square footage, two stories and years of buildup also add to the price. You get one flat price confirmed before we schedule.

Does a canal-front home in North Miami Beach need different cleaning than one inland?

The method is the same for most surfaces. We pressure wash concrete and pavers, and soft wash roofs, stucco, paint and screens. The difference is timing. Waterfront homes collect salt and algae faster, so they usually need a visit every few months instead of once a year. Recurring plans of 2x or 4x a year cost less per visit and keep the buildup from setting in.
