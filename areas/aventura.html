Why Aventura Needs It

Salt Air Never
Stops Working

Aventura sits between the Intracoastal and the ocean, and that position is the whole story for exterior maintenance. Salt-laden air settles on every surface continuously, leaving a fine gray film that most owners mistake for paint simply ageing. Add the density of association-managed property - from the Country Club Drive towers to the gated communities off Biscayne - and exterior standards here are enforced more tightly than almost anywhere in the county.

South Florida Climate

Miami-Dade and Broward average over 60 inches of rain per year. Humidity and warmth keep algae, mold, and mildew growing on exterior surfaces year-round.

Association Standards

Aventura is dominated by condo and homeowner associations with written appearance standards and active management. Common areas, walkways, garages and building exteriors are inspected routinely, and tenants are usually responsible for their own frontage.

Surface Types

Paver drives and motor courts, extensive common walkways, screened balconies and terraces, painted high-rise stucco, and pool decks in constant use. Coastal salt film needs soft washing with solution, not a harder rinse.

Pressure washing in Aventura: what's different here

Aventura sits between the Intracoastal Waterway and the Atlantic, with high-rise condo buildings, Williams Island and the Turnberry area. Salt air settles on glass, stucco and walkways every day, leaving a fine gray film that looks like age but washes off.

What removes the gray salt film on Aventura stucco and walls?

A soft wash. We apply a cleaning solution at low pressure, let it work, and rinse it off. That lifts salt, mildew and city grime without damaging stucco or paint. A house soft wash starts at $321, and your flat price is confirmed before we schedule. Salt returns, so a recurring plan at 1x, 2x or 4x a year keeps the film from building back up. Walkways, pool decks and garages are cleaned with regular pressure washing, while stucco and paint get the soft wash.
