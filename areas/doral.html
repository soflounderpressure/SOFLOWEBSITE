Why Doral Needs It

Doral Properties
Take a Beating

Doral's rapid growth has made it one of Miami-Dade's most active residential markets. With master-planned communities like Doral Isles, Landmark, and Morgan Falls - all with active HOAs - keeping your property clean isn't optional.

South Florida Climate

Miami-Dade and Broward average over 60 inches of rain per year. Humidity and warmth keep algae, mold, and mildew growing on exterior surfaces year-round.

HOA compliance

Doral's HOA communities send violation letters fast. A bi-annual or quarterly cleaning schedule keeps you ahead of notices and protects your home's value in a competitive market.

Surface Types

Paver driveways, concrete walkways, stucco exteriors, and pool decks are standard across Doral's residential developments - all prone to algae and mildew in the South Florida heat.
