Why Dania Beach Needs It

Salt, Boats,<br>And Older Stock

Dania Beach combines three things that each change the job: an older residential housing stock with original painted surfaces, a working marine and boatyard industry along the Dania Cut-Off Canal, and the antique district's commercial frontage along Federal Highway. Add proximity to both the ocean and the airport, and surfaces here carry salt film plus general grime at a rate most inland Broward property does not see.

🌧️

South Florida Climate

Miami-Dade and Broward average over 60 inches of rain per year. Humidity and warmth keep algae, mold, and mildew growing on exterior surfaces year-round.

📋

Marine & Commercial Work

Boatyard aprons, marine service areas and antique district storefronts all need commercial treatment — hot water for oil and grease, and scheduling that works around trading and yard operations.

🏡

Surface Types

Older painted stucco and block, concrete drives and aprons, marine yard surfaces with oil staining, commercial sidewalks and storefront frames, plus docks and seawalls. Older paint needs soft washing.
