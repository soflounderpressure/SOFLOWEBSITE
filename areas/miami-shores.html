Why Miami Shores Needs It

Old Trees,
Older Houses

Miami Shores is one of Miami-Dade's oldest planned villages, and it shows in two ways that matter here. The tree canopy along the residential streets is mature and dense, so surfaces stay shaded and damp. And much of the housing stock dates to the 1920s through the 1950s - original stucco, older paint, barrel tile that has been up a long time. Neither is a candidate for high pressure.

🌧️

South Florida Climate

Miami-Dade and Broward average over 60 inches of rain per year. Humidity and warmth keep algae, mold, and mildew growing on exterior surfaces year-round.

📋

Village Standards

The Village of Miami Shores maintains genuine appearance expectations, and neighbors notice. Mildew streaking on light stucco and leaf-stained drives are the two things most commonly flagged.

🏡

Surface Types

Original stucco and painted masonry, aged barrel tile, brick and paver drives under constant leaf drop, and mature landscaping tight against the house. Leaf tannin staining is routine here and needs treatment rather than force.
