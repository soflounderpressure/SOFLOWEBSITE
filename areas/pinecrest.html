Can you clean tile roofs, travertine and paver pool decks without damaging them?

Yes, with the right method. Roofs get a soft wash, which means low pressure and a cleaning solution, because a hard spray can lift tile. Pavers can lose joint sand under a hard blast and travertine can pit or etch if the wrong cleaner touches it, so we use controlled pressure and a solution matched to the stone. When a surface is unclear, we test a small spot first.
