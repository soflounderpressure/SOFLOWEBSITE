Why Hollywood Needs It

Hollywood Properties
Take a Beating

Hollywood, FL spans everything from beachfront condos to suburban single-family neighborhoods. The proximity to the ocean accelerates salt-air damage and algae growth, while the city's mix of residential and commercial properties creates demand for both home and business cleaning.

South Florida Climate

Miami-Dade and Broward average over 60 inches of rain per year. Humidity and warmth keep algae, mold, and mildew growing on exterior surfaces year-round.

HOA compliance

Hollywood's residential communities and beachside developments maintain active HOA standards. For commercial properties along Hollywood Blvd and Young Circle, exterior appearance is key to attracting customers.

Surface Types

Hollywood properties range from oceanfront stucco exteriors to suburban driveways and commercial storefronts - all surfaces we regularly clean throughout the city.

Pressure washing in Hollywood: what's different here

Hollywood runs from the Broadwalk and beachside condos to older neighborhoods like Hollywood Lakes and Driftwood, where single-family homes sit on real yards. The beach side takes the most salt. Gray film shows up on stucco, railings and walkways faster than it does a few miles inland.

Older homes usually mean original paint and stucco, and those get a soft wash, not high pressure. Driveways, walkways and patios get pressure washing. We match the method to the surface, so one visit can cover both. Rinsing alone rarely lifts salt film, so we treat the surface first and then rinse. Painted and stucco walls get the gentler method.

Can you clean my Hollywood property before I sell or rent it?

Yes. Hollywood has plenty of rentals and resales, and a clean exterior makes a place look better in photos and in person. A typical prep visit covers the driveway, walkway, patio and a house soft wash. Extra surfaces in the same visit are discounted, and we can usually schedule within the week.
