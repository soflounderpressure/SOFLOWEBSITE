Why Weston Needs It

Weston Enforces
Appearance

Weston is one of the most comprehensively master-planned cities in Broward, and exterior appearance standards here are stricter and more consistently enforced than almost anywhere else we work. Communities through Bonaventure, Weston Hills and the Savannah and Ridges villages all operate written standards covering driveways, roofs and exterior walls. Properties back onto extensive lakes and preserve, which keeps humidity high and growth steady year-round.

🌧️

South Florida Climate

Miami-Dade and Broward average over 60 inches of rain per year. Humidity and warmth keep algae, mold, and mildew growing on exterior surfaces year-round.

📋

HOA Enforcement Is Real Here

Weston's associations are active and organised. Roof streaking and driveway staining are the two most commonly cited items, and deadlines on violation notices are generally taken seriously by management.

🏡

Surface Types

Paver and concrete driveways, barrel and flat tile roofs, painted stucco, screened pool enclosures, and extensive perimeter walls. Lakefront and preserve-backing lots stay damp and grow faster.
