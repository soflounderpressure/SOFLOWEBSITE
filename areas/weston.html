What's different about cleaning in Weston

Weston is a master-planned city, and many homes sit in gated communities with written appearance standards. Many homes have tile roofs, painted stucco, paver or concrete driveways and screened pool enclosures. Lakes, preserve and irrigation keep moisture high, and many lots back onto water, so roofs, walls and driveways often streak or grow algae that shows from the street. Tile and stucco do well with a low-pressure soft wash, and the goal is an even, clean look that keeps the original color.

HOA fines and violation notices in Weston

Many Weston homeowners belong to a homeowners association, and a dirty roof, a stained driveway or streaked walls are often what a notice is about. Under Fla. Stat. 720.305, fines are generally capped at $100 per violation, up to $1,000 for a continuing violation, and the association must give notice and hold a hearing before a committee first. This is not legal advice, so check your own governing documents. If you have a letter, read what to do about an HOA violation letter for a dirty roof.
