Why Coral Springs Needs It

Water Is
Everywhere Here

Coral Springs was master-planned in the 1960s and built around lakes, canals and neighborhood after neighborhood of homes that share the same rules. That layout is great for living and hard on exterior surfaces. Lake and canal lots hold moisture in the air, sprinklers hit fences and pool decks every morning, and stucco walls stay damp long enough for algae and mildew to settle in. Fences go gray, pool decks turn slick, and tile and concrete roofs streak black.

South Florida Climate

Miami-Dade and Broward average over 60 inches of rain per year. Humidity and warmth keep algae, mold, and mildew growing on exterior surfaces year-round.

HOAs & City Standards

Much of Coral Springs sits inside an HOA, and many of those associations send notices over mildew on stucco, stained driveways and dirty fences. A clean exterior is the easy way to stay off that list.

Surface Types

Painted stucco, wood and vinyl fences, concrete and paver pool decks, screened enclosures, and tile and shingle roofs. Roofs and stucco get a low-pressure soft wash. Concrete and pavers can take more pressure.

Pressure washing in Coral Springs: what's different here

Coral Springs is a planned city. It was chartered in 1963 and developed by Coral Ridge Properties, which is why so much of it is organized into distinct communities with their own entrances, landscaping and rules. Neighborhoods such as Eagle Trace, Heron Bay, Ramblewood and the gated Wyndham Lakes each have their own look, and many have an association that cares how fences, driveways and roofs look from the street.

The property mix is mostly single-family homes and townhomes with stucco walls, privacy fences and a lot of screened pool areas. Rain and humidity do the rest. Even a clean fence can pick up a green film within a few months of the wet season, and north-facing walls and shaded roof valleys go first.

Can you clean a wood or vinyl fence and a pool deck in Coral Springs in the same visit?

Yes, and it is the combination we get asked about most here. Fences and pool decks are both outdoor surfaces that stay wet, so they turn green on the same schedule. We soft wash wood and vinyl fence panels so the boards are not scarred, then clean the pool deck with a surface cleaner and a mildew treatment that leaves a non-slip finish. Booking both together costs less than two separate visits. If you want the results to last, a recurring plan of 2x or 4x a year costs less per visit.

Many Coral Springs homes have a roof that needs attention too. Tile and shingle roofs are cleaned with a low-pressure soft wash, never a blast, so the surface is not damaged.
