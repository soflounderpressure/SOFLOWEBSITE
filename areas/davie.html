What's different about cleaning in Davie

Davie is known for its equestrian roots, and many lots are larger than a typical suburban lot, with ranch-style homes, long driveways, wide patios and long fence runs. Exterior pressure cleaning here usually means a few surfaces in one visit, such as the house, driveway, patio and fence.
