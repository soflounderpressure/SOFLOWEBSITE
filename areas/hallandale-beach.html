Why Hallandale Beach Needs It

Towers, Salt,
And Shared Space

Hallandale Beach is dominated by condominium towers along the ocean and the Intracoastal, which makes most of the work here association work: shared walkways, garages, pool decks, building exteriors and common areas rather than individual driveways. Salt air is constant, and on high-rise exteriors it builds a gray film that is only visible from a distance - which is usually when a board member raises it.

South Florida Climate

Miami-Dade and Broward average over 60 inches of rain per year. Humidity and warmth keep algae, mold, and mildew growing on exterior surfaces year-round.

Association Common Areas

Most Hallandale property is association-managed with shared responsibility for common areas. Recurring contracts covering walkways, garages and pool decks on a schedule cost meaningfully less per visit than reactive cleaning.

Surface Types

Building stucco and rendered exteriors, garage decks and ramps, extensive common walkways, pool surrounds and terraces, railings and balcony undersides. Coastal film needs solution, not a harder rinse.
