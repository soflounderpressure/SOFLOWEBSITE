Why South Miami Needs It

Older Homes,
Busy Sidewalks

South Miami mixes decades of housing stock - 1940s and 50s bungalows, mid-century ranches, and newer infill - with one of the most walkable downtown districts in Miami-Dade. That means two very different jobs in one city: careful soft washing on older painted surfaces in the residential streets, and hot-water sidewalk work along Sunset Drive where foot traffic, grease and gum accumulate continuously.

🌧️

South Florida Climate

Miami-Dade and Broward average over 60 inches of rain per year. Humidity and warmth keep algae, mold, and mildew growing on exterior surfaces year-round.

📋

Residential & Commercial Both

The Sunset Drive district has real commercial demand - restaurants with sidewalk seating and storefronts where the frontage is the first thing a customer walks across. We schedule commercial work before opening so nobody loses a trading day.

🏡

Surface Types

Older painted stucco and timber on the residential side, plus concrete sidewalks, entryways and awning frames downtown. The commercial sidewalks need hot water; cold pressure just moves grease around.
