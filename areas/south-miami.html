Why South Miami Needs It

Older Homes,
Busy Sidewalks

South Miami mixes decades of housing stock - 1940s and 50s bungalows, mid-century ranches, and newer infill - with one of the most walkable downtown districts in Miami-Dade. That means two very different jobs in one city: careful soft washing on older painted surfaces in the residential streets, and hot-water sidewalk work along Sunset Drive where foot traffic, grease and gum accumulate continuously.

South Florida Climate

Miami-Dade and Broward average over 60 inches of rain per year. Humidity and warmth keep algae, mold, and mildew growing on exterior surfaces year-round.

Residential & Commercial Both

The Sunset Drive district has real commercial demand - restaurants with sidewalk seating and storefronts where the frontage is the first thing a customer walks across. We schedule commercial work before opening so nobody loses a trading day.

Surface Types

Older painted stucco and timber on the residential side, plus concrete sidewalks, entryways and awning frames downtown. The commercial sidewalks need hot water; cold pressure just moves grease around.

Pressure washing in South Miami: what's different here

South Miami is small, and the housing changes a lot from one block to the next. Streets in the Twin Lakes area are full of one-story ranch homes from the mid-century years. Closer to downtown there are older bungalows and newer homes squeezed onto the same blocks. Painted stucco is the common thread, and old paint is the thing to protect.

Near Sunset Drive and US-1, traffic and foot traffic leave grime on walls and walkways. Properties on busy streets tend to need a rinse more often than the ones tucked back on quiet blocks.

We confirm one flat price before we schedule. See your South Miami price in a few taps.

Will pressure washing peel the paint on an older South Miami home?

It can, if the wrong method is used. Old paint on stucco loses its grip over the years, and a hard stream gets under it. For painted walls we soft wash, which means low pressure and a cleaning solution that lifts mildew and dirt without blasting the surface. If the paint is already flaking, we will tell you before we start so you know what to expect.
