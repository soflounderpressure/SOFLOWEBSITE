Pressure washing in Hialeah: what's different here

Pressure washing in Hialeah starts at $119 for a driveway, $149 for a patio or pool deck and $349 for a roof soft wash. We confirm one flat price before we schedule, with same-week scheduling.

Most Hialeah homes are single-story concrete block, many built decades ago, with tile or flat roofs, iron fences and enclosed front porches. Block and stucco are porous, so algae and mildew settle in and show up as gray and black streaks, mostly on shaded walls.

Lots are often close together and driveways run right up to the house, so a dirty driveway and a dirty front wall tend to go together. Fences and gates pick up grime too. We match the method to the surface: soft wash for painted block, stucco and roofs, pressure washing for concrete. Faded walls show dirt fast, and a fresh clean often makes a house look recently repainted.

Can you wash a tile or flat roof in Hialeah without damaging it?

Yes. Roofs get a soft wash, which means low pressure and a cleaning solution instead of a hard spray. High pressure can lift tile and chew up flat-roof coatings. The solution kills the algae that causes black streaks, and the water just rinses it off. We would rather tell you what a roof needs than guess, so ask first.
