Why Hialeah Needs It

Hialeah Properties
Take a Beating

Hialeah is one of the largest cities in Florida and home to thousands of single-family homes and residential properties. The dense urban environment, combined with South Florida's heat and rain, makes algae, mildew, and grime buildup a constant issue.

South Florida Climate

Miami-Dade and Broward average over 60 inches of rain per year. Humidity and warmth keep algae, mold, and mildew growing on exterior surfaces year-round.

HOA compliance

Many Hialeah neighborhoods have active HOAs, and city code enforcement inspects for exterior maintenance regularly. Staying on a cleaning schedule protects you from notices and fines.

Surface Types

Concrete driveways, flat roofs, stucco exteriors, and metal fences are the norm in Hialeah - all surfaces we clean regularly throughout the area.

Pressure washing in Hialeah: what's different here

Most Hialeah homes are single-story concrete block, many built decades ago, with tile or flat roofs, iron fences and enclosed front porches. Block and stucco are porous, so algae and mildew settle into the surface and show up as gray and black streaks, mostly on shaded walls.

Lots are close together and driveways often run right up to the house, so a dirty driveway and a dirty front wall tend to go together. Fences and gates pick up grime too. We match the method to the surface: soft wash for painted block, stucco and roofs, pressure washing for the concrete. Older block homes with unpainted or faded walls show dirt fast, and a fresh clean often makes a house look recently repainted.

Can you wash a tile or flat roof in Hialeah without damaging it?

Yes. Roofs get a soft wash, which means low pressure and a cleaning solution instead of a hard spray. High pressure can lift tile and chew up flat-roof coatings. The solution kills the algae that causes black streaks, and the water just rinses it off. Roof soft wash starts at $349. We would rather tell you what a roof needs than guess, so ask first.
