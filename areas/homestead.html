Pressure washing in Homestead: what's different here

Pressure washing in Homestead starts at $119 for a driveway, $149 for a patio or pool deck and $349 for a roof soft wash. We confirm one flat price before we schedule, with same-week scheduling.

Homestead sits at the southern edge of our service area, near the Redland farm country, with an older downtown and historic district surrounded by newer subdivisions. Homes near farm fields often pick up more dust and dirt, and warm, wet weather keeps algae growing on top of it. Driveways, walkways and fences usually show it first: dust sticks to damp concrete, turns into a gray crust, and mildew grows through it. Roofs on newer homes can start to streak sooner than owners expect. A home near a field can need a different plan than one in a downtown block, so tell us where the dirt is coming from.
