Why Kendall Needs It

Kendall Properties
Take a Beating

Kendall stretches across a large swath of suburban Miami-Dade and is home to some of the county's most active residential markets. Large lots, long driveways, and pool decks mean plenty of surface area that needs regular attention.

South Florida Climate

Miami-Dade and Broward average over 60 inches of rain per year. Humidity and warmth keep algae, mold, and mildew growing on exterior surfaces year-round.

HOA compliance

Kendall communities including The Hammocks, The Falls area, and Killian Green have active HOAs. Keeping up with South Florida's growth cycle keeps you off their radar.

Surface Types

Kendall homes typically feature long paver or concrete driveways, large pool decks, barrel tile or shingle roofs, and wood or vinyl fences - all standard services we provide.

Pressure washing in Kendall: what's different here

Kendall is a big suburban stretch, and the homes vary a lot by neighborhood. Kendale and Kendale Lakes have homes from the 1960s through the 1990s, while The Hammocks and newer pockets are later builds. Older stucco and shingle or tile roofs have had more years to collect algae.

Mature trees are the other story here. Oaks and mango trees shade driveways and roofs, drop leaves and fruit, and keep surfaces damp. Shade is where green and black growth starts first, and it comes back sooner than on an open lot. Older Kendall homes with original driveways often have stained, rough concrete that cleans up better than owners expect.

Is a yearly cleaning plan worth it for a Kendall home?

If your yard has shade, usually yes. Shaded surfaces regrow fast, so one deep clean can look tired in months. Plans run 1x, 2x or 4x a year, and each visit costs less than a one-time job. A home under big trees often does well on 2x. Call and we will suggest what fits your lot. The visit price drops the more often we come, and we confirm it before any plan starts.
