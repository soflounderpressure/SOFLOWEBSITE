Why Miami Beach Needs It

Miami Beach Properties
Take a Beating

Miami Beach's ocean-side environment is beautiful - and brutal on exterior surfaces. Salt air accelerates algae and rust staining on driveways, stucco, and roofs faster than anywhere else in South Florida. Regular cleaning isn't a luxury here; it's maintenance.

🌧️

South Florida Climate

Miami-Dade and Broward average over 60 inches of rain per year. Humidity and warmth keep algae, mold, and mildew growing on exterior surfaces year-round.

📋

HOA Compliance

Miami Beach condo associations and HOAs maintain strict exterior standards. Salt staining and mildew buildup on common areas and individual units is a regular compliance issue.

🏡

Surface Types

Stucco exteriors, concrete driveways, pool decks, and rooftop terraces are standard across Miami Beach's mix of single-family homes, condos, and commercial properties.
