What's different about cleaning in Coral Gables

What is the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution, and it is the method for roofs, stucco and painted surfaces. Pressure washing is for hard surfaces like concrete driveways and pavers. We pick the method after looking at the surface.

Can you clean coral rock walls and stone driveways?

Yes, with a gentler approach. Porous stone can be damaged by a hard spray, so we choose the method for the surface first. If a wall is already loose or crumbling, tell us when you call so we can plan for it before the day.
