Why Fort Lauderdale Needs It

A City Built<br>On Water

Fort Lauderdale has more navigable canal frontage than anywhere else in Broward, and water is the defining maintenance factor. Canal-front homes through Las Olas Isles, Rio Vista and Victoria Park carry constant humidity off the water on top of ordinary South Florida growth, and docks, seawalls and pool decks all sit permanently damp. Inland, the older Victoria Park and Sailboat Bend housing stock brings original surfaces that need soft washing rather than pressure.

🌧️

South Florida Climate

Miami-Dade and Broward average over 60 inches of rain per year. Humidity and warmth keep algae, mold, and mildew growing on exterior surfaces year-round.

📋

Waterfront &amp; Historic Both

Canal-front property needs more frequent attention than inland, and the older neighbourhoods need gentler method. Commercial work along Las Olas and the downtown frontage is scheduled outside trading hours.

🏡

Surface Types

Docks, seawalls and pool decks, paver motor courts, barrel tile and flat roofs, painted stucco and original older masonry, plus commercial sidewalks and awning frames. Dock and seawall areas grow the fastest on any waterfront property.
