Your Info,
Plain and Simple.

What we collect, what this site remembers on your device, and how to clear it.

What we collect when you ask for a quote

When you use the instant quote or the contact form, we collect your name, phone number, email, property address or ZIP, and the job details you pick (surfaces, size, condition, timing). We use it for one reason: to text or call you back with a flat price and to schedule the work if you say yes. We do not use it for anything else.

If you tap "Continue with Google" on the quote, Google shares your name and email with us so we can fill in the form. We do not receive your password or anything else from your Google account, and you can type your details instead.

What this site saves on your device

To save you time, this site stores a few things in your own browser (localStorage), not on our servers: the area you picked, an unfinished quote, and where you came from (for example a Google search or an ad). The area and where you came from are kept up to 90 days. An unfinished quote is kept up to 30 days. That lets you come back and pick up where you left off.

You can clear all of it any time by clearing site data for sofloup.com in your browser settings. Private browsing windows clear it when you close them.

Measurement tools

We may use Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta (Facebook) and Microsoft Clarity to see which pages people visit, which ads bring in requests, and where people get stuck. These tools may set their own cookies or identifiers. Your browser settings and the privacy controls in your Google, Meta and Microsoft accounts let you limit them.

What we never do

We never sell your information. We do not share it with anyone except the tools above and the people who help us run the business, and only as needed to do the job.

Questions or removal requests

Email SoFlo.Underpressure@gmail.com or call or text (305) 894-6336. If you want your quote request deleted, tell us and we will remove it.
