Why We
Started This

South Florida is tough on property. The combination of humidity, heat, salt air, and relentless rain means homes here need regular maintenance just to keep up. We started SoFlo UnderPressure because we saw a lot of properties that just needed a proper clean - and a lot of homeowners getting overcharged or let down by people who didn't care about the work.

We keep things simple: show up on time, do the job right, treat the property with care, and charge a fair price. No corporate call center, no subcontracted strangers. You deal directly with us from the first call to the last rinse.

We focus on residential and small commercial jobs - driveways, patios, roofs, fences, house exteriors, and storefronts. Nothing so big we can't give it proper attention. Mostly Miami-Dade, some Broward.

What We're About

Right Pressure, Right Surface

Not everything should get blasted at max PSI. We adjust pressure and technique for each surface - travertine, tile, wood, stucco - so nothing gets damaged.

You Deal with Us Directly

No call centers, no middlemen. You talk to the person doing the work. That means faster answers, real accountability, and someone who actually cares how it turns out.

Clean Is What We Leave Behind

We don't consider a job done until the surface is clean and the work area is cleared. No mess left on the lawn, no overspray on your car, no corners cut.
