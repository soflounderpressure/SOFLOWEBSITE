Soft Washing & Roofs

What is the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Pressure washing cleans with force - 2,000 to 4,000 PSI - and suits hard surfaces like concrete and pavers. Soft washing cleans with a solution at about 100 PSI, roughly garden-hose pressure, and is the only safe method for roofs, stucco, paint, siding, screens and wood. Force removes dirt; chemistry kills living algae. Most Miami homes need both, on different surfaces.

Is soft washing safe for my roof?

Yes - it is the method roofing manufacturers actually endorse. At around 100 PSI nothing is scoured or displaced, so no shingle granules come off and no tile is cracked or lifted. Pressure washing a roof, by contrast, voids most manufacturer warranties.

Why does my Florida roof have black streaks?

Those streaks are Gloeocapsa magma, a living algae that feeds on the limestone filler in shingles and the porous surface of tile. The black color is a UV shield it produces. It spreads by airborne spores and runs downward as rain carries it down the slope, which is why the staining looks like vertical streaks.

Can you pressure wash stucco?

You should not. Stucco is porous by design, so high pressure forces water into the wall and can blow out sections of the finish. Soft washing cleans it thoroughly without driving anything into the wall - it is the correct method for stucco, painted masonry and siding.

Driveways, Decks & Surfaces

Will pressure washing stripe or damage my driveway?

Not if a surface cleaner is used. Striping comes from cleaning with a handheld wand, which leaves overlapping arcs. We use a commercial surface cleaner - a round unit that rides flat and cleans a wide, even path - on every flat surface, which is what prevents striping.

Can you remove oil stains from a driveway?

Usually, to a degree. We pre-treat oil with a degreaser and let it dwell before washing. Fresh spills often come out completely; old, deeply penetrated oil generally lightens rather than vanishing. We tell you which to expect when we quote rather than promising a result we cannot deliver.

Can you clean travertine without damaging it?

Yes, at controlled low pressure with a surface cleaner kept moving. Travertine is softer and more porous than concrete and etches permanently under aggressive pressure. Acidic cleaners must also be kept off travertine, marble and limestone - they etch the stone chemically, and that damage cannot be reversed.

Can you pressure wash a wood fence?

Wood should be soft washed, not pressure washed. Pressure carves out the soft grain between the harder rings and leaves a furry, splintered surface that cannot be undone without sanding the whole fence. Low pressure plus a solution cleans it without wrecking the grain.

Scheduling & Service

How often should I clean my property in South Florida?

Driveways and patios every 6 to 12 months, house exterior once a year (twice near the coast), roofs every 1 to 2 years, and fences about annually. South Florida's rain, heat and humidity grow algae year-round, so surfaces here need cleaning far more often than in drier climates.

Do I need to be home during the service?

Not necessarily. As long as we can reach the areas being cleaned and access a water source, most jobs are completed without you present. We confirm everything is done and, for recurring plans, simply keep to the schedule.
