Annual Service Agreement

This agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between SoFlo UnderPressure Pressure Washing ("Company"), a pressure washing service operating in Miami-Dade and Broward County, Florida, and the property owner identified below ("Customer").

1. Service Plan & Commitment

Customer selects a recurring service plan as described on the Company's website and pricing sheet. All plans represent a 12-month service commitment beginning on the date of the first scheduled service visit. The selected plan, visit frequency, and service scope will be confirmed in writing by the Company prior to the first service date.

2. Payment Terms

Upon agreement execution, the Company will provide Customer with a secure payment link to set up automatic monthly billing via Stripe. The monthly payment amount is calculated by dividing the Customer's custom annual service rate by 12. Billing begins on or near the date of the first service. Customer authorizes recurring monthly charges to the payment method provided at signup for the duration of the 12-month term.

Payments are due on the same calendar date each month. Accounts with failed payments will receive a 3-day courtesy notice before services are paused. A 3% late fee applies to invoices more than 7 days past due.

3. Pricing & Custom Quote

All rates are custom-quoted based on property size, surface types, and service scope. The Company will provide a written quote following property review. Customer's signature on this agreement does not commit the Customer to any rate until the written quote has been reviewed and approved by both parties. No billing begins until Customer confirms the final quoted rate.

4. Scheduling

The Company will contact Customer in advance to coordinate each service visit. Customer must provide reasonable access to the property (open gates, moved vehicles, water access). If access is unavailable on the scheduled day, the Company will reschedule at no additional charge up to one time per visit.

Customer may reschedule any visit by notifying the Company at least 24 hours in advance. Same-day cancellations without notice may count as a completed visit for billing purposes.

The Company reserves the right to reschedule visits due to severe weather or equipment issues. Such reschedules will be communicated promptly and rescheduled within 10 business days.

5. Service Guarantee

If Customer is not satisfied with the results of a service visit, the Company will return within 72 hours to address any missed areas at no additional charge. Guarantee claims must be reported within 24 hours of the service visit via call or text to (305) 894-6336.

6. Early Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice via email or text. If Customer terminates within the first 6 months of the agreement, an early termination fee equal to one (1) remaining monthly payment will apply. After 6 months, Customer may terminate with 30 days notice and no additional fees. The Company may terminate for non-payment after a 10-day cure period.

7. Damage & Liability

The Company takes care to perform all services without causing damage. Customer agrees to notify the Company of any pre-existing surface conditions, fragile materials, or special care instructions prior to service. The Company is not responsible for damage to surfaces that are already cracked, eroded, or in a deteriorated state that is not reasonably visible. Claims for damage must be submitted within 48 hours of service.

8. HOA Documentation

Upon request, the Company will provide a service completion certificate suitable for HOA compliance purposes. This documentation is included at no extra charge for Standard and Premium plan customers.

9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida. Any disputes shall be resolved in Miami-Dade County, Florida.

10. Electronic Signature

Customer acknowledges that typing their full legal name in the signature field below constitutes a legally binding electronic signature under the Florida Electronic Signature Act (F.S. § 668.001 et seq.) and the federal E-SIGN Act. Customer confirms they have read and understood this entire agreement.
