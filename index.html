Questions we get a lot

How much does pressure washing cost in Miami?

Most residential jobs are priced flat, not hourly. Starting prices: driveways from $119, patios and pool decks from $149, house exterior soft wash from $279, and roof soft washing from $349. Prices vary by area, and the instant quote shows yours. The final number depends on size, surface and condition. We give you the price up front before scheduling, and there is no surprise on the invoice.

How do I get a quote?

Use the instant quote for a price in a few taps, or text a few photos to (305) 894-6336. We review the property details and come back with a flat price. Bundling several surfaces into one visit - say a driveway and a house wash - costs less than booking them separately.

What is the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Pressure washing cleans with force - 2,000 to 4,000 PSI - and suits hard surfaces like concrete and pavers. Soft washing cleans with a solution at about 100 PSI, roughly garden-hose pressure, and is the only safe method for roofs, stucco, paint, siding, screens and wood. Force removes dirt; chemistry kills living algae. Most Miami homes need both, on different surfaces.

Do I need to be home during the service?

Not necessarily. As long as we can reach the areas being cleaned and access a water source, most jobs are completed without you present. We confirm everything is done and, for recurring plans, simply keep to the schedule.

What areas do you serve?

All of Miami-Dade and Broward County - from Homestead up through Miami, the Gables, the Beaches and Aventura, across to Doral and Hialeah, and throughout Broward including Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Davie, Weston, Coral Springs and Fort Lauderdale. See our service areas page for the full list.

Who actually shows up to do the work?

The owner. SoFlo UnderPressure is a one-truck, owner-operated company. The person who quotes your job is the person who cleans it. You agree a flat price before any work starts, we soft wash roofs and stucco so nothing gets damaged, and you get before and after photos when it's done.
